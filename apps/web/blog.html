Getting Started with Promota in 5 Minutes

15 October 2026 · 3 min read

You do not need a laptop, a bank balance or a large following to start. If you have a Nigerian phone number and about five spare minutes, you can create an account, verify it, and take your first brand-promotion task. This is the exact path from signup to your first reward.

  1. Create your account

    Sign up with your Nigerian mobile number and a password. The number is your identity on Promota — brands and the verification system key everything to it.

  2. Verify by OTP

    We send a one-time code by SMS or WhatsApp. Entering it confirms the line is yours and unlocks the task board, your wallet, and your invite code.

  3. Browse live tasks

    Filter the board by reward type — data or cash — network, and brand. Each task shows its proof rules and reward before you take it, so nothing is a surprise later.

  4. Do the task and submit proof

    Complete the promotion exactly as briefed, then upload the screenshot or public link the task demands. Read the proof rules twice; they define what gets approved.

  5. Earn when it is verified

    Approved tasks credit your wallet. Redeem MTN, Glo or Airtel data whenever you like, or cash out to your bank from Level 2 after KYC.

Three habits that get first-time tasks approved

  • Pick tasks that fit your real audienceA WhatsApp status task only works if your contacts actually see your status. Match the task to the platform you genuinely use.
  • Keep your screenshot cleanCapture the full post — handle, timestamp and engagement visible. Cropped or edited images fail automated checks.
  • Read rejection reasonsEvery rejection comes with a reason in your dashboard. Fix that one thing and the resubmission usually clears.

That is the whole loop: verify, take a task, submit proof, get paid on approval. Everything else — referral levels, analytics, higher-paying campaigns — builds on a verified account.

How Promota Keeps Your Work Safe: Verification, Disputes, and Fair Play

13 October 2026 · 4 min read

The fairest system on a promotion platform is one where proof matters more than volume. Promota is built so that every paid result traces back to evidence a human can check, and every disagreement has a written path to resolution. Here is how that works in practice — for promoters and for the brands funding the work.

Proof submission: what we ask for and why

Each task publishes its proof rules before anyone takes it: the exact screenshot, the public link, or the WhatsApp status capture it requires. When you submit, automated checks flag recycled images, stale timestamps and duplicate links before a budget is spent. That first pass protects you as much as the brand — a genuinely completed task should never lose to a lazy one.

The dispute lifecycle

If your task is rejected and you believe the work was completed as briefed, open a dispute from your dashboard. A human reviewer — not the same automated pass — re-reads the task rules against your evidence. The reviewer can overturn the rejection, uphold it with a written explanation, or ask for one additional piece of evidence. Disputes are time-boxed; you are never left waiting without a status.

  • Step one: open the disputeState what you completed and attach anything the original submission missed. There is no fee to dispute.
  • Step two: independent reviewA reviewer who did not run the first check compares your proof to the brand's published rules.
  • Step three: decision on the audit trailThe outcome — overturned or upheld — is written to your audit trail with its reasoning, visible to you and recorded for the campaign.

Fair play cuts both ways

Brands get the same protection: recycled screenshots, multi-account submissions and off-brief work are flagged before they drain an escrow. Promoters get predictable rules, a real reviewer, and a record they can point to. Nobody pays for work that cannot be checked, and nobody loses paid work without a hearing.

For Businesses: Fund a Campaign, Pay Only for Verified Results

11 October 2026 · 4 min read

Most brand-promotion budgets fail in the gap between paying for posts and proving they happened. Promota closes that gap with an escrow model: you fund the campaign first, promoters do the work against published rules, and money moves only when a result passes verification. Here is how a campaign runs from setup to settlement.

Fund escrow, then publish tasks

Every campaign starts with a funded escrow in Naira. Tasks only go live against money already deposited, which means approved work never waits on an invoice and your budget cannot drift mid-campaign. You set the reward per task, the number of slots, and the cities or nationwide reach — Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt or everywhere.

Write proof rules promoters can actually follow

The strength of a campaign is the clarity of its proof rules. Specify the exact evidence before promoters start: a screenshot showing your handle and timestamp, a public link that stays live for a set period, or a WhatsApp status captured within the task window. Vague rules produce disputes; precise rules produce approvals.

  • Per-task budgets stay ring-fencedEach task draws only from its campaign escrow, so one oversubscribed task cannot eat another's reward pool.
  • Live submission trackingYour dashboard shows approvals, rejections and dispute outcomes as they happen — per task and per promoter.
  • Fraud checks before paymentDuplicate screenshots, recycled posts and multi-account patterns are flagged before they spend your budget.
  • Unspent escrow returnsSlots that never fill, or work that never verifies, revert to you when the campaign closes.

Track performance, settle cleanly

During the campaign you can see completion rates and approval times per promoter, which makes repeat bookings straightforward. Disputes are reviewed against your published rules by an independent reviewer, and every outcome lands in the campaign's audit trail. When the campaign ends, you have paid for verified distribution — not for promises — and a clear record of exactly what ran, where, and to whom.

Referral Levels Explained: How Your Network Grows Your Earnings

9 October 2026 · 3 min read

Every Promota account carries an invite code. Used well, that code does two things: it brings people you trust onto the platform, and it raises your referral level, which unlocks better-paying tasks and better reward rates. This post explains exactly how levels work — and just as importantly, how they do not.

What counts as a qualified referral

A signup alone does not count. A referral becomes qualified when two things happen: the person verifies their phone number with the OTP, and they complete their first verified task. That bar exists for a reason — it means your level reflects people who actually do brand-promotion work, not dormant signups.

The level ladder

  • Verified referrals move you upEach qualified referral moves you toward the next referral level. Levels are cumulative and permanent — one quiet month does not reset your standing.
  • Higher levels unlock better tasksSome campaigns and higher reward rates open up as your level climbs, because brands prefer networks with a track record of approved work.
  • Your referrals level tooThe people you invite climb the same ladder on their own referrals. You are not capped by your own network size.

Why this is not a pyramid scheme

The distinction matters, so here it is plainly: nobody pays to join Promota, and nobody earns money simply for recruiting. There is no joining fee, no buy-in, and no payout that depends on downline payments — every naira and every data bundle on the platform traces back to a verified, brand-funded task. Recruitment without task work earns nothing, by design. The referral system rewards you for bringing in people who do real promotion for real brands; it is a growth mechanic attached to a task marketplace, not an income-opportunity scheme.

Grow it the honest way: share your code with people who already post on WhatsApp, X or Instagram — classmates in Abuja, colleagues in Lagos, creators whose work you rate. Qualified referrals follow.

Why We Pay in Data Bundles: Rewards That Actually Matter in Nigeria

7 October 2026 · 3 min read

Ask a creator in Surulere or a campus promoter in Ibadan what they need to keep working, and the honest answer is rarely complicated: data. Airtime and data bundles are the working capital of online promotion in Nigeria. That is why Promota settles verified tasks in MTN, Glo and Airtel data by default — with naira cash available from Level 2.

Data is a reward people can use today

A data bundle lands on the line you already use. There is no bank account requirement, no transfer delay, and no waiting for a batch window — redemptions are usually fulfilled within minutes of approval. For promoters who have not completed KYC or who simply do not want cash routed to a bank, data removes the friction that stops other platforms' rewards from ever being real.

Data funds the next task

Here is the part that matters most: promotion costs data. Posting the content a brand briefs, uploading proof screenshots, checking the task board — all of it runs on the same bundles the platform pays out with. When your reward is data, your earnings and your working costs net out in one place, and a light week on tasks never leaves you unable to work the next one.

  • Choose your networkRedeem on MTN, Glo or Airtel — match the bundle to the line you actually post from.
  • Cash unlocks as you level upFrom Level 2, after KYC, you can request bank payouts in Naira instead. Same verified tasks, different settlement.
  • Every redemption shows its statusYour wallet lists each request and where it stands, so nothing disappears into a queue you cannot see.

Both reward types sit in the same wallet, earned the same way: complete a task, pass verification. Data simply reflects how promotion actually works in Nigeria — on phones, on bundles, day to day.

How Promota Works: Your Guide to Earning from Brand Promotion

5 October 2026 · 4 min read

Promota is a task marketplace built for one job: connecting Nigerian brands that need genuine distribution with creators and promoters who can deliver it — and settling the exchange only when the work is verified. If you have ever promoted a brand for exposure alone, or paid a creator and hoped the screenshots were real, this is the system designed around both problems.

The task marketplace

Brands fund a campaign in escrow, then publish tasks with explicit proof rules: a WhatsApp status that stays up for a set period, a public post carrying the brand handle, a share with a screenshot attached. Each task states its reward, its network or city focus, and exactly what evidence it demands. Promoters filter the live board — by reward type, brand, or difficulty — take what fits their audience, and complete the work on their own platforms.

Verification before payment

Proof submission is where most promotion platforms hand-wave. Here it is the centre of the system. Automated checks flag duplicates and stale submissions first; then a human reviewer confirms the evidence matches the task's published rules. Approved tasks credit the promoter's wallet. Rejected tasks come with a written reason — and a dispute window if the promoter believes the work was completed as briefed.

  • Brands fund firstBudgets sit in escrow before a single task goes live, so approved work is always already paid for.
  • Promoters prove, then earnRewards — MTN, Glo or Airtel data bundles, or Naira from Level 2 — release only after verification.
  • Referral levels reward good networksVerified referrals unlock better tasks and rates, on top of earnings that come purely from task work.

Who it is for

For creators and part-time promoters in Lagos, Abuja and everywhere else with a phone: a free, transparent board where clear proof rules mean your finished work gets paid. For businesses: a way to buy verified distribution instead of impressions you cannot audit. The marketplace runs Nigeria-only for now — Naira settlements, local networks — and every campaign, dispute and redemption leaves a trail both sides can read.
