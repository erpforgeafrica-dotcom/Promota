About Promota

Promota connects Nigerian creators and businesses through verified brand-promotion work — paid in mobile data and cash.

Why we exist

Brands need real people sharing real posts, not bot reach. Creators already promote products they like — usually for nothing. Promota sits in the middle: brands fund escrow-backed campaigns with explicit proof rules, promoters complete tasks, and only verified results get paid.

How we operate

Every paid post carries a disclosure tag. We never buy fake likes, follows or views. Referral levels reward genuine promotion; they are not a pay-to-join scheme. Cash payouts follow Nigerian KYC requirements, and data rewards settle through licensed aggregators on MTN, Glo and Airtel.

Current status

Promota runs as a Nigeria-only pilot. Promoters join free; businesses fund campaigns in Naira. Product updates land on the blog, and policy details are published in full under Legal.
