Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-08 | Last updated: 2026-10-08

1. Data Controller

Promota is the data controller. Contact: privacy@promota.ng

2. Data We Collect

From Promoters (Registration)

  • Full name
  • Phone number (Nigerian MSISDN, normalized to 080xxxxxxxx format)
  • Optional note (city, preferences)
  • Referral code (if invited)
  • UTM parameters (source, medium, campaign, content, term)
  • Consent timestamp (client-side + server-side)
  • Honeypot field (bot detection, discarded)

From Verified Promoters

  • Verified phone status (OTP verification via Termii)
  • Network detection (MTN/Glo/Airtel/9mobile)
  • Task submissions, completion status, verification outcomes
  • Points balance, level, referral count
  • KYC Tier (T0/T1/T2) and age verification status (for cash unlock)
  • Identity reference (provider token or HMAC of NIN/BVN â€” never raw ID)
  • Wallet balance, redemption history (data/cash)

From Referrals

  • Referrer's invite code (masked name only)
  • Referee's phone + referral points earned

3. Legal Basis (NDPA)

  • Consent (s.27): Registration requires explicit, timestamped consent. Honeypot and 30-minute freshness window enforced.
  • Contract (s.28): Verified promoter data processed to assign tasks, verify completions, award points, process redemptions.
  • Legal Obligation (s.29): KYC/AML records retained per CBN/SEC requirements for cash payouts.
  • Legitimate Interest (s.30): Fraud prevention, trust scoring, abuse controls, referral tracking.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • Operate the promoter queue and referral system
  • Assign and verify tasks, award points
  • Calculate level progression and referral requirements
  • Process data bundle redemptions (via licensed aggregator)
  • Process cash redemptions (KYC Tier 2+, batched payouts via Flutterwave)
  • Prevent fraud (honeypot, consent freshness, unique phone, trust score, human review)
  • Resolve disputes and enforce anti-MLM rules
  • Comply with NDPA, SEC, and financial regulations

5. Data Sharing

  • Termii (SMS/WhatsApp OTP): Phone number only, for verification.
  • Data Aggregator (MTN/Glo/Airtel bundles): Phone + network only, for fulfillment.
  • Flutterwave (Cash payouts): Bank details only, for cash redemption.
  • KYC Provider (Smile Identity/VerifyMe): Identity documents, for cash unlock verification.
  • Legal/Regulatory: When required by Nigerian law or court order.

We never sell personal data. Referral codes show masked inviter name only (First Last-initial).

6. Data Retention

  • Registration data: 2 years from last activity, or until deletion request.
  • Verified promoter data: 7 years (financial records) or until deletion request.
  • KYC/AML data: 7 years per CBN regulations.
  • Dispute records: 3 years after resolution.
  • Audit logs: 7 years (append-only, immutable).

8. Security

  • Supabase Row Level Security (RLS) on all tables â€” anon cannot read private data.
  • Double-entry ledger with deferred balance constraints.
  • Immutable audit log for all sensitive operations.
  • Strict CSP, nosniff, frame-deny, referrer-policy: no-referrer.

9. International Transfers

10. Children

Promota is not directed to persons under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. Age verification is required for cash payouts.

11. Changes

We will notify registered users of material changes via email and update the effective date above.
